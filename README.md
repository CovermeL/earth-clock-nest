# World Time Studio

Design a premium, modern world clock and time zone website homepage, with a clean, minimalist aesthetic. Use a bright white background, soft gray accents, rounded cards, subtle shadows, and the Inter font. Center a large, live digital clock, show the current date, time zone, UTC offset, and daylight saving status. Add a search bar for cities and countries. Then, a responsive grid of world clocks for major cities. Include sections for time zone converter, daylight saving time info, sunrise and sunset times, and popular cities. Reserve three tasteful display ad placements that blend in, not distract. Make it mobile first, SEO optimized, and accessible, with generous white space and subtle animations.
